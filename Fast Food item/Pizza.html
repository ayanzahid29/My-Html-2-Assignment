Fajita


  • Fajita pizza is a popular fusion dish combining traditional pizza with the flavors of Mexican chicken or steak fajitas. It typically features a pizza dough base topped with salsa (or creamy sauce), fajita-seasoned grilled chicken or steak, sautéed bell peppers and onions, and melted mozzarella or Monterey Jack cheese











Cheese


  • A cheese pizza is a timeless culinary classic featuring a leavened dough base topped with a savory tomato sauce and a generous layer of melted cheese, usually shredded low-moisture mozzarella. Celebrated for its elegant simplicity, a perfect cheese pizza balances a crispy yet chewy crust with a vibrant, tangy sauce and a rich, gooey cheese layer that stretches beautifully with every bite











Creamy


  • A creamy pizza, also known as a "white pizza" (pizza bianca), replaces the traditional acidic tomato marinara with a rich, velvety dairy base. It features a dough brushed with garlic oil, topped with heavy cream, Alfredo, or seasoned ricotta, and loaded with melted mozzarella and fresh herbs











Malai Boti


  • A Malai Boti Pizza is a rich South Asian fusion pizza that combines a traditional Italian pizza crust with the creamy, smoky flavors of Indian/Pakistani Mughlai cuisine. It features tender chunks of marinated "Malai" (cream) chicken, a velvety white sauce or spicy mayo base, and fresh vegetables.











Bar BQ


  • A Bar BQ (BBQ) Pizza is a bold, American-style twist on classic Italian pizza. It swaps traditional tomato sauce for a sweet, smoky, and tangy barbecue sauce base. The pizza is most famously topped with tender pieces of grilled or shredded chicken, gooey melted cheeses, and sharp red onions. Originally popularized by California Pizza Kitchen in the late 1980s, this dish perfectly balances sweet heat with savory richness.











Vegetation


  • A vegetarian pizza is a colorful, meat-free pie built on a crisp dough base, layered with rich marinara, creamy garlic, or pesto sauce. It is loaded with fresh, roasted, or sautéed vegetables, topped generously with melted cheeses, and often garnished with fresh herbs.
