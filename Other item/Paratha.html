Pori Paratha

  • 100 Rs

    • A Pori Paratha (or Puri Paratha) is a delicious, deep-fried flatbread that combines the airy, puffed texture of a traditional poori with the rich, flaky layers of a paratha. It is a popular breakfast and barbecue staple across South Asia, featuring a crispy exterior and a soft, chewy interior.








Chai Paratha

  • 60 Rs

    • A chai paratha is a beloved South Asian culinary duo consisting of a hot, flaky, pan-fried flatbread (paratha) paired with a steaming cup of spiced, milky tea (chai). It is the ultimate comfort food, deeply ingrained as a classic breakfast or evening snack across Pakistan and India.
