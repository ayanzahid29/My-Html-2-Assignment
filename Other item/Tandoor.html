Naan

  • 50 Rs

    • A Sada Naan (plain naan) is a classic South Asian leavened flatbread known for its soft, pillowy interior and slightly chewy texture. Baked in a traditional clay oven (tandoor), it is served piping hot, often lightly brushed with butter or ghee, making it the perfect scoop for curries, kebabs, and lentils.








Rogni Naan

  • 80 Rs

    • Roghni Naan is a rich, soft, and fluffy Pakistani leavened flatbread, characterized by its golden, glossy top, sesame seed topping, and a savory, yogurt-and-milk-based dough. "Roghni" originates from roghan (clarified butter/ghee), signifying a luxurious bread, often baked in a tandoor, that is thicker and richer than plain naan.
