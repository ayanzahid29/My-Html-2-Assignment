Reshmi Kabab

  • 800 Rs (1 Plate)

    • Reshmi kabab is a popular, mildly spiced South Asian dish made from minced or boneless chicken marinated in yogurt, cream, cheese, and cashews. Its name translates to "silky" or "velvety," describing the extremely tender, melt-in-your-mouth texture achieved by slow-grilling. It is a staple at local BBQ spots.
