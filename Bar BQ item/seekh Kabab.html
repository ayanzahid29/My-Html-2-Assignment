Seekh Kabab

  • 600 Rs (1 Plate)

    • Seekh kabab is a popular South Asian dish consisting of minced meat (lamb, beef, or chicken) seasoned with aromatic spices, herbs, onions, ginger, and garlic. Shaped into cylinders around metal skewers ("seekh") and traditionally grilled over charcoal, they are known for their smoky, tender, and juicy texture.
