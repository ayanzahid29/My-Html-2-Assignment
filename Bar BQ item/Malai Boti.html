Malai Boti

  • 700 Rs (1 Plate)

    • Malai boti is a popular South Asian dish featuring tender, bite-sized pieces of boneless chicken marinated in a rich mixture of cream, yogurt, ginger, garlic, and mild spices. The word malai translates to cream, and boti refers to meat chunks. It is prized for its velvety, melt-in-your-mouth texture.
