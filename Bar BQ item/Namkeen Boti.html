Namkeen Boti

  • 300 (1 Seekh)

    • Namkeen Boti is a traditional South Asian meat dish, originating from the rugged, mountainous regions of Northern Pakistan and Afghanistan. True to its name (which translates to "salty"), this dish features bite-sized pieces of meat—usually mutton, beef, or lamb—seasoned with a minimalist blend of salt, black pepper, and ginger-garlic paste. It is typically slow-cooked in its own natural fats and a small amount of oil, highlighting the authentic flavor of the meat rather than heavy spices.
